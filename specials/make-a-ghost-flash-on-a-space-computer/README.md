**Spooktober Day 3: Flashing Ghost** — part of a free Halloween coding week, Mon 26–Fri 30 October 2026.

Boo's escaped a haunted house and been caught in a net. Today he goes to space. Sort of.

Draw him on an 8×8 grid of lights — the same kind of screen that's on the little computers up on the space station. Fill him in one square at a time, then code him to flash. Drawing on a grid is sneaky maths: every light has a position across and a position down. The flash is two pictures swapped back and forth, like a flipbook.

It's still all blocks — no typing yet. Everything runs in a web browser. No downloads, no coding experience needed. About 30–60 minutes.
