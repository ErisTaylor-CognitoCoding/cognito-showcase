**Spooktober Day 1: Ghost Hunt** — part of a free Halloween coding week, Mon 26–Fri 30 October 2026.

Meet Boo, a little pixel ghost lost in a haunted house. Snap puzzle blocks together to guide him out — he does exactly what your blocks say, not what you meant.

Everything runs in a web browser. No downloads, no coding experience needed. About 30–60 minutes per day.
